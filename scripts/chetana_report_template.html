Zero Duplicate Speech jsPDF Engine
Single-Instance Neural Audio Synthesis

Eliminated speech audio repetition by introducing an incrementing generation counter (speechSeqRef). When user dialogue or quick chips are triggered, any prior active HTML5 audio instance is immediately halted. Web Speech fallback only executes if network audio fails.

  • English Engine: en-US-EmmaMultilingualNeural (warm, soothing therapeutic inflection).
  • Hindi & Hinglish: hi-IN-SwaraNeural with native Devanagari phoneme synthesis.
Dual-Formant Viseme & Acoustic SER Engine

The acoustic speech engine analyzes vocal pitch fundamental ($F_0$) via Normalized Autocorrelation (NACF) and pitch jitter ($J$). 2048-point FFT extracts formant peaks ($F_1, F_2$) mapped to lip-sync cels:

  • Vowel /a/ ($F_1 \approx 780\,\text{Hz}, F_2 \approx 1250\,\text{Hz}$) → Viseme "ah"
  • Vowel /i/ ($F_1 \approx 280\,\text{Hz}, F_2 \approx 2300\,\text{Hz}$) → Viseme "ee"
  • Vowel /u/ ($F_1 \approx 340\,\text{Hz}, F_2 \approx 850\,\text{Hz}$) → Viseme "oh"
Integrated EmoSense Session Report & PDF Export
